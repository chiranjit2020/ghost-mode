# My Strategic Recommendations Before We Generate Images

There are three things I would settle before moving into visual production.

**1. Separate the actual product from the fictional story.**

Ghost World can use devils, Pure Souls, purification, powers and cloaks as its fictional language. But the real product must clearly explain what happens to an account, its content and its privacy. In particular, purification should never expose real-world identity or automatically delete an account.

**2. Keep the reward system fair.**

The mystery of Devil Points is an interesting mechanic. However, publish the general rules, prevent manipulation and don't punish users for receiving positive reactions. Randomness should create surprise, not make users feel cheated.

**3. Don't build every feature before launch.**

For the first release, I would prioritise:

- Ghost identity and profiles.
- Posts, comments and reactions.
- Devil Points.
- Devil ranks.
- Dark Feed.
- Pure Soul encounters.
- Soul Exposure.
- Shadow Cloak.

The remaining features can arrive through subsequent releases.
